India

The Land of Incredible Heritages

A great country that predominantly occupies parts of South Asia with its unique history and amazing diversity varying from landscape, wildlife, culture, and traditions

With the massive territory, India, the world’s second-most populous country and the seventh-largest country by area boast about its rich tapestry of natural and cultural heritage. The great South-Asian country hosts multiple religions co-existing for centuries that assimilated and gave birth to the unique experience called India. A wide range of religious and historical monuments, magnificent temples and churches, grandiose fort and palace complexes, and even holy sites in Indian’s spiritual world, etc, all have created incredible cultural enlightenment for anyone visiting the country. Not only famous for its variety of religious and historic highlights, but India also welcomes nature lovers to discover the country’s natural treasures including numerous wildlife sanctuaries or beautiful dreamy beaches to the South. India’s notable heritages promise you a fabulous journey with endless fun and unforgettable experiences from the beginning to the end.

Highlights in our India Tours

Our best India Tours collection focuses on bringing the real local experiences and unmissable activities to provide an unforgettable trip to travellers.

A Rich Tapestry Of Cultural Diversity

India features an iconic and vibrant cultural identity with its religious variety that is well-reflected through countless cultural sites across the country from majestic temples, mosques, to Catholic churches or basilicas, etc. They could stand for different religions, but all have made a perfect blend of Indian culture existing for centuries that surely invites anyone who is culture buffs.

Incredible Historical Monuments

Been through a long-lasting and great history of different empires as well as colonization, India boasts about such a huge collection of historical monuments that many among of which are the world-famous heritages. The incredible fusion of styles from different cultures, along with their architectural grandeur made them excellent ways to learn about the history and culture of India.

Land Of The Myths

The spiritual world is a notable highlight that no one could miss when travelling to India. Myths and worships play a significant role in the lives of people in the South-Asian country. Witnessing and admiring the revered & up-lifting ambience of India’s holy sites, ancient Aarti Ceremony on the bank of the mystic Ganges River, or daily rituals of locals, etc, would be unforgettable travel experiences for you.

Exclusive Wildlife Beauties

India is endowed with incredible coverages of forested areas and Ecos-regions where is the home to many endemic & endangered species. Some of the notable national parks and natural reserves of India are the world’s finest place to see special wild animals such as Bengal Tigers, Asiatic Lions & Great One-horned Rhinos in their natural habitat. Hence it is no wonder that India is a promising land of wildlife viewing for any enthusiasts.

Gastronomic Highlights

As one of the most popular cuisines in the world, Indian culinary art is the special medley of cultural variety. Sundry ingredients, tongue-tickling flavours, and signal cooking styles created an amalgamation of spices, scents, and colours for Indian food. Through exciting foodie trips that we offered, sampling some of the famous dishes here is already a must in your India travel bucket list.

Multiple Beach Activities

For those who are seeking a wonderful beach getaway, India also hosts numerous beautiful sandy beaches where offer almost everything for a stupendous vacation soaking up the sun-sand-sea when in the country. From the high-end services to budget stays, from delicate wellness options to common beach activities, Indian beaches make sure your outings full of fun from dawn till dusk.

Distinct Local Life

As the home of people from different religions and cultures, India owns its charms of an exotic local life that you could hardly find anywhere. Hopping on a joyful rickshaw ride in bustling Old Delhi, experiencing houseboat stay in Kerala, tribal village visits, or witnessing traditional rituals are just a few of many distinct local delights that we’re offering during your amazing India holiday!

The best time to take India Tours is from late October to the end of April when the weather is more likely to be sunny, dry and warm. During this time, the north of India offers clear blue skies. However, please carry a light jacket with you in December and January when the weather is much cooler with potential fog. The summer months from May to October can get unbearably hot.

FAQs about India Tours

Cash or credit card in India?

It is possible to use credit cards in India’s major cities or famous tourist attractions. Cash (Indian rupee), of course, is widely used anywhere in the country. In case you’re visiting smaller towns of less-visited regions, payments by credit cards could be a narrow choice. Hence, it is recommended that you should already have enough an amount of Indian currency for purchasing or tipping purpose.

Is the tap water in India clean to drink?

There is a fact that Indians do not drink off the tap directly. Tap water here is still commonly used for daily hygiene such as washing, bathing or brushing teeth though. Overall, to make sure that you’re well when travelling in India. Bottled, filtered, boiled water is highly recommended to drink.

What is India's preferred currency?

The Indian rupee (INR) is India’s national and also preferred currency. Hence payments using any other currency are nearly impossible in the country. Major international currencies such as US dollars, British pounds and Euros can be easily exchanged throughout India. Most international airports of the country, major banks, or authorized money changers have the money exchange counters, and some selected bigger hotels and resorts also have this facility for customers.

Best India SIM card to buy

Getting a SIM card in India for foreign tourist requires preparing a lot of documents and there are many steps involved in the process. Three best and most consistent mobile data providers in India are Jio, Airtel, and Vodafone. Foreign tourists here are suggested to use online social networks such as Facebook and Whatsapp to save costs. The main point is, even if you manage to acquire an Indian SIM Card, the number of public internets and wifi hotspot zones in India are very limited.

Is the food in India safe and clean?

Cultural diversity has created variations of Indian cuisine and also the common local delicacies in a different region. Meat dishes are very popular in northern zones while vegetarian cuisine is most favoured in southern parts. Seafood is also available in India, especially in coastal regions. Overall Indian food is safe and clean to eat if these dishes are prepared in restaurants and cafeterias. As a precaution, be more aware when trying any street food across the cities in India.

Is tipping mandatory in India?

Tipping is not a cultural tradition or common etiquette in India. However, people could expect you to tip in most of the tourist attractions. Normally, a service fee is automatically added to the bill in most bigger hotels and restaurants. It is highly recommended that you should tip at various places and instances depending on your satisfaction level. You can leave a personal tip for those who gave you an excellent service and made you satisfied.

Which destinations should I visit in India?

The best answer should be related to your travel plan. While the major tourist attractions such as Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Mumbai, Varanasi, and Amritsar offer you the real essence of India in history and cultural heritage, some of the notable national parks & wildlife reserves in Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Assam are the dreamlands for nature and wildlife enthusiasts. To the south of the country hosts the city of Goa, Tamil Nadu and Kerala state where own numerous interesting cultural experiences and beach activities that worth visiting if you’re more into the lesser-known destinations in India.

What is the best time to visit India?

Covering a greater part of South Asia, India features a variety of weather conditions, hence you will find different weather in different states of India at the same time of the year. The ideal time to visit India depends on your planned destination and activities you want to experience. With a tropical climate, summertime in India might be very hot. Hence winter months from October to March, when the weather is pretty mild and the temperature is pleasant, is the best time to plan a trip to India (especially Northern and Central regions). If you’re visiting India in summer, Southern zones, with inviting sandy beaches and cool weather, are likely to be a perfect part of your travel plan.

How long do I need to explore India?

India is a huge country both geographically and culturally. Hence it could be difficult for anyone to travel the entire country for just one time. Normally, a classic itinerary encompassing the most-visited tourist hotspots will take from 10 days to 3 weeks depending on your travel plan. If you’re expecting an in-depth exploration of India’s highlights from North to South, from West to East, you need at least 2 months for both traversing time and visiting time in each destination.

Where should I visit in India on my 1st-time tour routes?

If you’re travelling in India for the first time, the most popular and iconic tourist destinations of the country are undoubted must be included in your trip. Some of Indian sought-after attractions that are commonly preferred by most first-timers are the capital city Delhi, the state of Rajasthan, the land of majesty Agra with the world-famous heritage Taj Mahal, and the holy city of Varanasi in Uttar Pradesh, where houses numerous significant monuments, grandiose forts & palaces, and showcases typical India’s ways of life. Many also incorporated the famed Golden Triangle: Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur in their first itinerary to catch the most classic glimpse of India.
