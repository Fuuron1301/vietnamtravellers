# Product Context

register: product

## Product Purpose
Ha Long Luxury Travel is a headless luxury travel CMS and frontend platform. WordPress remains the native admin for editors, sales and operators. Next.js consumes validated content through REST APIs and renders SEO hub pages, luxury tour pages and tailor-made booking flows.

## Users
- Admins managing CMS structure, SEO and governance.
- Editors entering tours, countries, travel styles, blog posts and translations.
- Sales staff reviewing leads and bookings.
- Travelers using SEO hub pages and tailor-made forms.

## Strategic Roots
- Asia Pioneer Travel: destination menu and country hub architecture.
- Asiatica Travel: prominent Tailor-made / Customize Your Trip conversion flow.
- LuxTravel DMC: luxury visual identity, large imagery, refined travel positioning.

## Product Principles
- WordPress admin must stay native and fast.
- Next.js must receive clean, predictable, SEO-safe data.
- Invalid tour data must be visible to editors but hidden from public listings.
- Governance should help editors fix content without replacing WordPress UI.
